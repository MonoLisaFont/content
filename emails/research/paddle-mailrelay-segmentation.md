# Mailrelay segmentation for MonoLisa Black Week 2026

**One person can receive at most one Black Week email across all four drafts.** Mailrelay deduplicates a subscriber who appears in several groups selected for a single delivery. Each draft here is a separate delivery. Assign each person to one draft before scheduling any of them, and carry that assignment through retries. [Mailrelay sending guide](https://help.mailrelay.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000424825)

## Who gets which email

Start with active, sendable subscribers in the intended MonoLisa newsletter audience. Include eligible subscribers in **MonoLisa customers** (group 1) and people who joined the newsletter directly. Respect unsubscribes, blocks and other Mailrelay suppressions. A trial download or an opt-in to trial setup tips alone does not grant newsletter consent. [Checkout audience and consent](../../../website/docs/mailrelay-customers.md)

Use all active orders for a person. Apply the exclusions below first, then take the **first matching row**. A later trial download or purchase of an older version does not override a higher version the person owns.

| Priority | Active Paddle Classic products (production IDs) | Campaign tag | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | v3 Developer Code `925531`, without v3 Text | `bw-2026-v3-code` | [Add Text to Code](../drafts/black-week-2026-v3-code.md) |
| 2 | v2 Basic `798586`, Plus `798587`, or Complete `798588` | `bw-2026-v2` | [Upgrade from v2](../drafts/black-week-2026-v2.md) |
| 3 | v1 Personal `578353`, Plus `638029`, Plus Upgrade `638030`, or Professional `580502` | `bw-2026-v1` | [Upgrade from v1](../drafts/black-week-2026-v1.md) |
| 4 | Current Trial `925530` or legacy Trial `661578`, with no active paid license | `bw-2026-trial` | [Get the full Code + Text bundle](../drafts/black-week-2026-trial.md) |

Exclude people who own the v3 bundle (`925532`) **or** both standalone families (Code `925531` and Text `926417`). My Orders recognizes both paths. Leave v3 Text-only owners, Creator owners (`925533`), and active Indie, Company or Enterprise subscribers (`765053`, `772304`, `772305`) out of these four sends, including when they also have an older order. Their licenses need different copy. Leave newsletter-only contacts and unresolved product or identity matches unassigned. ## Build the recipient list

1. **Reconstruct ownership.** Export the full Paddle Classic Transactions history across all versions, including zero-value trials and completed free upgrades. Read order ID, order status, product ID and customer email. Match refunds by order ID and include relevant manual invoices separately. Count completed and partially refunded orders. Disregard fully refunded or disputed orders and deleted subscriptions, then re-evaluate the person's remaining orders. A v2 Complete owner who qualifies for a free upgrade stays in v2 until there is an actual v3 order. [Transactions report](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/reporting/a-comprehensive-insight-into-the-transactions-report) · [Refunds report](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/reporting/understanding-the-refunds-report) · [Order entitlements](../../../website/app/orders/orderEntitlements.ts)
2. **Resolve one person to one subscribed address.** Page through existing Mailrelay subscribers. Trim and lowercase emails for exact matching, then combine records across addresses only when a verified customer or account link connects them. Names, dots and plus-addresses alone are insufficient. Among a person's eligible addresses, prefer their designated newsletter address, then the most recent explicit newsletter opt-in, then the lowest subscriber ID as a stable tie-breaker. Hold conflicting identity or consent records and report unmatched orders separately. The rule covers verified aliases. Two addresses with no known link can still represent the same person. [Subscriber lookup](https://apidocs.mailrelay.com/tag/subscribers/GET/subscribers)
3. **Dry-run all four assignments together.** Keep a private campaign roster with one row per person. Record the person key, selected Mailrelay subscriber ID, normalized delivery address, assigned tag or exclusion reason, and send status. Use the normalized address as the person key for an exact single-address match and one shared key for verified aliases. Keep recipient rows and exports outside Git. Report counts for each variant, exclusions, held matches, and proposed tag additions and removals. The existing checkout backfill provides CSV parsing and a dry-run pattern. Its post-v3 enrollment cutoff would miss historic owners. [Existing backfill](../../../website/docs/mailrelay-customers.md)
4. **Create the four Mailrelay segments.** Create the four tags above. Use the complete roster to remove stale Black Week tags, including from aliases, and give each selected subscriber exactly their assigned tag. Under **Subscribers → Segmentation**, create one segment per draft. Each must match newsletter eligibility **and** its exact tag. Select only that segment when preparing its newsletter. Keep persistent ownership tags separate from the campaign tags. [Tag conditions](https://help.mailrelay.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000411315-tags) · [Segmentation guide](https://help.mailrelay.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000156746) · [Add tags](https://apidocs.mailrelay.com/tag/subscribers/POST/subscribers/%7Bsubscriber_id%7D/tags) · [Remove tags](https://apidocs.mailrelay.com/tag/subscribers/DELETE/subscribers/%7Bsubscriber_id%7D/tags)

## Verify and send once

- **Check the actual membership.** Read back the four tagged audiences and compare their member IDs with the roster. All six pairwise intersections must be empty by person ID, Mailrelay subscriber ID, and normalized address. The sum of the four segment sizes must equal the number of distinct assigned people. Compare each Mailrelay recipient preview with its expected count after suppressions. Resolve differences before scheduling.
- **Spot-check overlaps.** Trial + v1 + v2 gets the v2 email. V2 + v3 Code gets the v3 Code email. Code + Text bought separately gets no email, including when a verified alias holds the second purchase. V2 + standalone v3 Text gets no email. A newsletter subscriber with only a legacy trial gets the Trial email. A trial-tips-only contact gets no email.
- **Freeze assignments before the first delivery.** Mailrelay segments update as subscriber data changes. After the freeze, changes can only remove recipients. Before each delivery, drop anyone who has unsubscribed, purchased the target, or otherwise ceased to qualify for their assigned copy. During this campaign those people stay out of subsequent segments. [How Mailrelay segments work](https://help.mailrelay.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000156746)
- **Reserve once across all four sends.** Use one sending operator and one roster for `black-week-2026`. At launch or restart, reconcile any existing Black Week deliveries under **Reports → Sent campaigns**. Before submitting each variant, reserve its recipients in the roster. Save the Mailrelay delivery ID when scheduling or queueing succeeds. Anyone already reserved, scheduled, queued or sent is ineligible for another delivery in this campaign.
- **Recover the original delivery on a retry.** After a timeout, pause or partial failure, look up the recorded delivery and resume it where possible. A replacement send may include only people confirmed never queued in any of the four deliveries. Hold uncertain rows and use delivery records to reconcile them. Opens and clicks cannot establish who was queued. [Sending and delivery reports](https://help.mailrelay.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000424825)

## Keep ownership current for later campaigns

For recurring campaigns, maintain ownership independently of these four Black Week tags. Extend the signed Paddle Classic webhook to recompute a buyer’s state from all relevant orders after a successful payment or refund. Reconcile before each campaign to pick up disputes, missed events and later newsletter signups. Use the permanent `Owns MonoLisa v3 Developer + Text` tag for either path to Code + Text. Compare fully paginated Classic API results with a complete export before using them to remove ownership tags.
