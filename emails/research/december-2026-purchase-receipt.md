# December newsletter purchase reminder

## Suggested P.S.

P.S. Bought MonoLisa for work this year? In My Orders, open your purchase and select **Receipt** under **Amount** to get a copy for your records. If you’re self-employed, the work-related cost may qualify as a business expense under your local tax rules. [Find my receipt in My Orders](https://www.monolisa.dev/orders)

## Editorial note

Run this in December, when Black Week buyers have a receipt to file. Eligibility and how buyers claim the cost depend on where they file and how they use the purchase.
