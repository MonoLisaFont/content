## Attribution and review boundaries

The full chapter's factual outline and timestamps were checked against the public video's chapter metadata and automatic English captions. Video samples around 1:14:11, 1:20:42, 1:30:30, and 1:38:00 were also inspected to confirm the website tester, enlarged letterforms, Text specimen, and Roboto comparison. The draft does not describe the review as independent or make sponsorship claims; the source checked here does not establish that relationship. The host's preferences about width, reading comfort, and enjoyment should remain attributed opinions if added to the email.
